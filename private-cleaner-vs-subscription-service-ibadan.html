Private Cleaner vs. Subscription Cleaning Service in Ibadan: Pros, Cons & Cost Comparison

Comparing private independent cleaners with subscription-based cleaning services in major Nigerian cities—evaluating costs, vetting, supervision, and peace of mind.

6 min read • Published by Cleanse.ng Editorial • Ibadan, Nigeria

Homeowners in Ibadan looking to delegate domestic cleaning usually face a choice between two main options: hiring a private independent cleaner on a daily/pay-per-visit basis or subscribing to a managed cleaning subscription service like Cleanse.ng.

While hiring an independent cleaner might seem cheaper on paper, evaluating the true cost requires factoring in cleaning supplies, transit allowances, background vetting, and your own managerial time. Here is an objective comparison to help you choose the best option for your lifestyle.

1. Hiring a Private Cleaner in Ibadan

A private cleaner is an independent individual hired directly by the homeowner to clean on specific days of the week.

Pros of Private Cleaners:

  • Low upfront entry cost per day (typically ₦4,000 – ₦8,000 cash wage).
  • Personal relationship directly with one individual.

Cons of Private Cleaners:

  • No Supervision: You must stay home to oversee their work and re-clean missed spots yourself.
  • Unpredictable Hidden Costs: You are responsible for buying all cleaning detergents, bleach, mops, microfibers, and transit stipends.
  • No Vetting or Insurance: Informal hiring lacks background checks or damage coverage if property is misplaced or damaged.
  • Frequent Cancellations: If your cleaner is sick or unavailable, your home remains uncleaned with zero backup support.

2. Using a Subscription Cleaning Service (Cleanse.ng)

A subscription service provides a professional team, active on-site supervision, and all equipment under a flat, predictable monthly plan (twice a week).

Pros of Subscription Services:

  • Dedicated On-Site Supervisor: A supervisor oversees every visit, ensuring flawless quality without your involvement.
  • 100% All-Inclusive Supplies: Professional equipment, detergents, and tools are brought by the team at no extra cost.
  • Vetted Staff & Business Insurance: Identity verification, background checks, and formal damage insurance policies.
  • Guaranteed Punctual Schedule: Fixed visits twice a week automatically without chasing or rescheduling hassles.

Cons of Subscription Services:

  • Higher upfront fee than paying a single day's cash wage (though monthly subscriptions offer up to 58% savings per visit).
