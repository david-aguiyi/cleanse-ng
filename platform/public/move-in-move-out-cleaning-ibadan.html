What's Included in a Move-in or Move-out Clean

An empty home shows everything. Your Cleanse Pro works through every room, inside and out, so the property is ready to hand over or move into:

Kitchen

  • Inside cupboards and drawers wiped out
  • Countertops, splashbacks and wall tiles degreased
  • Sink and taps scrubbed and sanitised
  • Outside of any appliances left in place cleaned
  • Kitchen floor degreased, corners included

Bathrooms

  • Toilet, basin, shower and bathtub scrubbed and sanitised
  • Tiles and grout scrubbed
  • Mirrors, taps and fittings polished
  • Bathroom cabinets wiped inside and out

Bedrooms & Wardrobes

  • Inside wardrobes and built-in cupboards wiped out
  • Wardrobe tops, shelves and ledges dusted
  • Window sills and skirting boards wiped
  • Floors cleaned edge to edge

Living Areas & Empty Rooms

  • Cobwebs cleared from ceilings and corners
  • Ledges, sills, door frames and window frames wiped
  • Dust and debris left from moving swept out
  • Every room checked, including stores and spare rooms

Fixtures & Fittings

  • Light switches and sockets wiped
  • Door handles and locks cleaned
  • Reachable light fittings dusted
  • Taps, mirrors and towel rails polished

Floors

  • All floors swept and mopped edge to edge
  • Corners and skirting lines detailed
  • Rubbish bagged and removed from rooms
  • A final walk-through before your Cleanse Pro leaves

Move-in vs Move-out Cleaning

Move-out clean

  • Done after your things are packed and moved
  • Gets the property ready to hand back
  • Helps tenants and landlords hand over clean keys
  • Leaves the home ready for the next occupant

Move-in clean

  • Done before your furniture and boxes arrive
  • Cupboards and wardrobes clean before you unpack
  • Clears dust and debris left by builders or past occupants
  • You step into a fresh home from day one

What is the difference between move-in and move-out cleaning?

Move-out cleaning gets a property ready to hand back, so tenants and landlords can hand over clean keys. Move-in cleaning gets a home ready before you unpack, so cupboards, wardrobes and floors are fresh before your things arrive. Both cover the same rooms and work best in an empty home.

Do you clean inside cupboards and wardrobes?

Yes. Inside kitchen cupboards, drawers and wardrobes are wiped out as part of a move-in or move-out clean, which is easiest when the home is empty.

Please tell us whether running water and electricity will be available on the cleaning day. It affects how we plan the visit, so we ask before confirming your quote.
