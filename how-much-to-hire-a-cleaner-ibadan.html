How Much Does It Cost to Hire a Cleaner in Ibadan?

A transparent breakdown of cleaner rates in Ibadan—comparing daily rates, agency fees, informal arrangements, and flat-rate professional subscriptions.

If you are looking to hire a house cleaner in Ibadan, understanding current market rates is essential to budgeting effectively. Whether you live in Bodija, Jericho, Akobo, Oluyole, Iyaganku, or GRA, cleaner costs vary based on whether you hire an independent daily worker, go through a placement agency, or subscribe to a professional service.

In this guide, we break down what you can expect to pay across different cleaner arrangements in Ibadan for 2026, highlighting the hidden costs that often catch homeowners off guard.

A low daily rate often masks significant hidden costs: purchasing detergents, mops, microfibers, paying transportation stipends, and spending your own weekend supervising the work.

The Hidden Expenses of Hiring Independent Cleaners

When you hire an unvetted daily cleaner in Ibadan, the sticker price is rarely your final cost. Consider these additional expenses:

  • Cleaning Supplies & Hardware: Expect to spend ₦10,000 to ₦15,000 per month on quality tile cleaners, bleach, disinfectants, mops, and microfiber cloths.
  • Transportation Allowances: Workers frequently ask for extra transit stipends on top of their day rate.
  • Uninsured Damage Risks: If a fragile mirror, countertop, or appliance is damaged during cleaning, you absorb 100% of the replacement expense.
  • Your Personal Time: Staying home to oversee the work and re-cleaning missed spots costs valuable hours every single week.
