A top-tier residential cleaning service should save you time, guarantee safety, provide active supervision, and operate with zero management effort required from you.

2

Traditional Domestic Nanny & Helper Agencies

Overview: Recruitment agencies that connect homeowners with live-in or live-out domestic maids for an upfront agency placement fee.

  • Staff Vetting: Basic guarantor forms, but high turnover rates.
  • Supervision: 0% (homeowner must supervise and manage daily tasks).
  • Supplies: Homeowner buys all cleaning agents and tools.
  • Pricing: High upfront placement fee + monthly salary + food/living overheads.

Verdict: Suitable if you need full-time cooking/childcare, but inefficient if you simply want a clean, well-maintained home without managerial stress.

3

Independent Freelance House Cleaners

Overview: Informal sole-proprietor cleaners hired via word-of-mouth recommendations from neighbors or friends.

  • Staff Vetting: None (relies entirely on personal trust).
  • Supervision: None (requires homeowner present during visit).
  • Supplies: Must be supplied by homeowner.
  • Pricing: Low per-visit cash price, but high risk of last-minute cancellations.

Verdict: Low budget entry option, but carries significant security risks, inconsistent quality, and frequent scheduling cancellations.

4

Commercial & Post-Construction Cleaners

Overview: Specialized industrial cleaning outfits focused on office buildings, post-construction cleanup, and fumigation.

  • Staff Vetting: Corporate employee vetting.
  • Supervision: On-site project manager for heavy jobs.
  • Supplies: Heavy industrial machinery and chemicals.
  • Pricing: Very high one-off project pricing (not designed for weekly home upkeep).

Verdict: Excellent for heavy post-building scrub-downs, but far too expensive and heavy-handed for routine residential maintenance.
