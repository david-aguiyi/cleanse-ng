The Jericho Home Maintenance Challenge

Whether you inhabit a multi-bedroom apartment near Jericho Mall or a expansive residential compound off Magazine Road, keeping a home pristine requires continuous effort. For busy professionals working long hours, domestic cleaning often creates a frustrating dilemma:

  • Unreliable scheduling: Informal domestic workers who cancel last-minute or show up unannounced when you are busy.
  • Security & Trust concerns: Allowing unscreened staff into your private living spaces without verifiable identity checks or insurance coverage.
  • Management Fatigue: Having to purchase cleaning supplies yourself, issue constant instructions, and re-clean missed spots afterwards.

Cleaning your home should not be what interrupts your creative focus, your career, or your family time. You hired help to free your mind, not to create another project to manage.
