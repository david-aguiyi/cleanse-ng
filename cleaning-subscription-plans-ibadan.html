1. How Cleaning Subscriptions Work in Ibadan

A cleaning subscription operates like utility automation: instead of booking a cleaner from scratch every time your home gets messy, you subscribe to a **monthly package of 8 visits (cleaned twice per week)** on fixed, guaranteed days.
